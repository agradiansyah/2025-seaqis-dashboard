# 2025 SEAQIS Training Alumni Dashboard
SEAQIS (South-East Asia Minister of Education Organization QITEP in Science) organizes training events for educators across Southeast Asia.
The goal of this project was to clean and integrate fragmented alumni data, then create a Tableau dashboard to visualize participation trends and event reach.
<br><br>
This dashboard helps SEAQIS:<br>
1. Track participant distribution across countries, provinces, and institutions.<br>
2. Identify gaps and unbalanced participation.<br>
3. Speed up research and reporting with clear, interactive visuals.<br>
4. Built with: Excel, Tableau Public<br><br>
Data Size: 1,900+ raw records, cleaned to 1,559 unique records<br>

## Problem
Before this project, SEAQIS data faced several issues: <br>
<img width="675" height="124" alt="Screenshot 2025-09-27 at 10 38 43" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1985e7ec-f9a8-4d48-8472-c310d564902e" />
1. Files were scattered across multiple sources.<br>
2. Inconsistent data formats for provinces, participant names, and institutions.<br>
3. Duplicate records inflated reporting numbers.<br>
4. Many missing fields, making analysis difficult.<br>
This made reporting slow and unreliable, preventing SEAQIS from understanding participation patterns.

## What We Did
<strong>Data Cleaning<br></strong>
Performed in Microsoft Excel:<br>
- Standardized province names, participant names, and institutions.<br>
- Removed duplicate entries for accurate reporting.<br>
- Combined multiple files into one clean dataset.<br>
- Final dataset: 1,559 unique alumni records ready for analysis.<br>
<strong>Why cleaning matters: <br></strong>
- Makes data uniform and reliable.<br>
- Ensures accurate and valid analysis.<br>
- Simplifies dashboard creation in Tableau.<br>

## Final Dataset
Key improvements:<br>
1. Institution names converted to uppercase for consistency.<br>
2. Added missing participant titles when matching records were found.<br>
3. Consolidated all events into one dataset for centralized reporting.<br>

## Key Findings
<strong>1. Participant concentration:<br></strong>
Jawa Barat dominated with 311 participants, followed by Riau (108) and Jawa Tengah (91).<br>
Other provinces had fewer than 30 participants.<br><br>
<strong>2. Top programs:<br></strong>
BIMTEK (220), REPDIS BOGOR (210), and PESERTA KS(Principal) (150) were most popular.<br>
Specialized programs like Robotics had low participation (45).<br><br>
<strong>3. Institution insights:<br></strong>
Dinas Pendidikan contributed the most participants (45).<br>
Top schools: SMA BPI (38) and SD BPI (33).<br>
Most other institutions had fewer than 16 participants.<br>

## Recommendation
1. Organize more training events outside Jawa Barat(West Java) to increase regional balance.<br>
2. Offer online training options to make programs more accessible for educators in remote areas.<br>

## Impact
1. Faster reporting: Research teams can now access insights directly from the dashboard, saving hours of manual work.<br>
2. Strategic planning: SEAQIS can target underrepresented regions and balance participation across Southeast Asia.<br>
3. Better decisions: Data-driven approach helps improve future event design and outreach.<br>
